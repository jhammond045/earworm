# Stage Q2: stretching the take's audio

Built Nov 22, 2026, from [quant.md](quant.md) "Build plan > Q2". A flexed clip's take audio now follows its anchors: the engine stretches the take between hard anchors without changing its pitch, so the raw voice or beat lands where Feel moved its notes and hits. Two modes, picked on the Feel row: **Hits** (slices, the default on beat layers) and **Smooth** (a phase vocoder, the default on melody and texture layers). Q1 left that audio at its performed timing. An audio file can now come in as a take, so a sample or a loop can be flexed. One layer driving others (Q3) and anchor editing (Q4) are not in this stage.

## What was built

| Part | Where |
| --- | --- |
| The stretcher: hits and smooth, anchor-locked as quant.md describes (5 ms pre-roll, 20 ms copied verbatim after each hard anchor, the last half frame at the take's own speed, nothing read past the next anchor, soft anchors only bend). Moved from the prototype as it was measured, less WSOLA, and made to loop: the map comes as runs of points folded into the clip's loop, and the loop start cuts. Every buffer is allocated when the engine starts. | `crates/klont/src/stretch.rs` (`Stretcher`) |
| One stretcher per layer plus a second one, so a new map, a new clip or a jump in the transport crossfades over 5 ms. A reload that leaves the clip's map as it was changes nothing. A tempo ramp into the section: the stretcher plays on at the section's tempo and catches up with the crossfade when the ramp ends. | `LayerStretch` in `stretch.rs`; `stretch_at` and the control tick in `engine.rs`; `RawSrc::Stretch` in `layer.rs` |
| Track blob v5 ("EWT5"): P3's v4 (a note curve per note), plus a flags word on every warp anchor (bit 0 hard, bit 1 starts a run) and the audio mode in clip word 15 (0 today's read, 1 hits, 2 smooth). v1 to v4 still load, and the encoder writes v5 only when a clip stretches, so every other project stays byte-identical. | `crates/klont/src/seq.rs`, `web/src/m1/track.ts` |
| The stretch map: the clip's quantized anchors with their take seconds, put in take order, placed where the time map plays them, and folded into the loop. A take placed across the loop end gives two runs; where a take longer than the loop would play over itself, the earlier part plays. Beat clips send theirs too (in Q1 their warp maps stayed in the document). Without anchors that know their take second, or past 1,024 points, the clip keeps Q1's performed timing. | `stretchMap`, `flexAudio` in `web/src/project/flex.ts`; `trackInput` in `project/compile.ts` |
| `Flex.audio`: `'hits'` or `'smooth'`, missing means by layer kind. The Feel row has an Audio menu, and its note says whether the raw input is stretched. | `project/types.ts`, `checkFlex`; `web/index.html`, `fillFlex` in `app/main.ts` |
| Export WAV renders at the audio context's rate instead of a fixed 48 kHz, so takes stretch in exports as they do live. | the export handler in `app/main.ts` |
| Import take: a button beside Record. The file is decoded at the engine's rate, run through the live tier in its own klont.wasm instance (the same audio and frames a capture of that sound gives), given to the second pass, and placed from the start of the section on the selected layer. A layer without an input element gets one at mix 1, so the take plays as audio. One undo step. | `web/src/project/import-take.ts`; `importFile` in `app/capture.ts`; `style.css` |
| The stretcher's cost in `bench --costs`. | `crates/klont/src/bin/bench.rs` |

### Listening

All 6 s, 48 kHz mono, in `docs/q2-examples/`. Each take's files share one gain. Listen to these first:

1. **`corpus-voiced-0-as-played.wav` against `corpus-voiced-2-strength-100.wav`.** A beatbox take from `beat_corpus` ("boom", "pop", "nnt") with a quiet click on every beat, quantized to 8ths in hits mode. Its anchors read 2.6 ms early on average, the case Q1 left to be judged by ear (question 1 there). If the hits sit on the click, Q1's anchor error is fine. `corpus-four-*` is the same with kick, hat, snare and pop (3.0 ms early).
2. **`sung-0-as-played.wav`, `sung-1-strength-50.wav`, `sung-2-strength-100.wav`.** The prototype's sung line (late, loose, with legato joins) to 8ths in smooth mode. Listen for the joins staying smooth and the pitch staying put.
3. **`drums-0-as-played.wav`, `drums-1-strength-50.wav`, `drums-2-strength-100.wav`**, then **`drums-smooth-2-strength-100.wav`.** Drum loop A (swung, a late snare) to straight 16ths in hits mode, then the same in smooth mode, to compare the two on drums.

## Where the build differs from the design

- **Track blob v5, not v4.** Polyphonic capture (P3) landed first and took v4 for note curves, so the warp anchor flags and the clip's audio mode are v5, which keeps v4's note curves.

- **The map comes folded into the loop.** quant.md gave the warp anchors a hard flag only. The engine needs to know where the take jumps (at the loop start, for a take placed across it), so a second flag starts a run, and the compiler folds the map into the loop before sending it.
- **Every stretch fades in over its pre-roll**, the first one in a pass too (the prototype's first segment had none). Play from a stop, or a new clip at a section change, starts at full level.
- **The take has to be at the engine's rate to stretch.** A take at another rate keeps today's read. Captures are at the audio context's rate and an import is decoded at it, so live playback always stretches. Exports used to render at 48 kHz whatever the context's rate; they now render at the context's rate, so on a 44.1 kHz machine an export stretches as live playback does. On a 48 kHz machine nothing changes.
- **A tempo ramp into a section.** The stretcher counts samples at the section's tempo, so during a ramp it plays at that tempo, up to a few tens of ms off the transport, and moves to where the transport is with the 5 ms crossfade when the ramp ends.
- **Map size.** A clip's stretch map holds up to 1,024 points, which keeps a layer's stretcher at 360 KB. A 59 s take of 16ths at 180 bpm has about 700. A clip with more keeps Q1's performed timing.
- **Short pieces.** Interior points within 12 ms of a run's ends are dropped, and pieces of a fold shorter than 6 ms are left out, so no three stretches overlap.
- **Memory is taken for all eight layers when the engine starts** (360 KB each), whether or not anything stretches.
- **The cost meter.** The stretcher is priced in `bench --costs` but not in the Sound panel's cost meter, which counts patch modules; a stretching layer does not lower its voice cap.
- **The attack check reads the prototype's mean.** In hits mode an anchor pulled early leaves the slice before it playing its own audio into the 2 ms before the attack, so the worst single attack over that window is 0.994. From the anchor on, every attack is 1.000.
- **Not done:** the reference laptop's cost (check 4), and the chord take (check 5).

## Questions for the owner

1. **Anchor accuracy by ear.** Do the corpus examples sit on the click? Recommended: if they do, keep the detector as it is; if the early "boom" and "pop" hits flam against the click, make an onset refinement for anchors before Q3.
2. **Reference laptop cost.** Could you run `cargo run -p klont --release --bin bench -- --costs` on the reference laptop and send the last three lines? Eight smooth layers are 4.5% of one build-machine core here.
3. **Smooth mode for voice (quant.md question 4).** Import now takes a real recording. Could you import a sung line and a beatbox loop of your own, quantize them, and say whether smooth mode sounds right on the voice? WSOLA stays unbuilt until then.
4. **Importing.** An imported take starts at the section start and the file is not trimmed. Recommended: keep that, since a loop exported from elsewhere starts on its downbeat. Or trim leading silence, as Influence does with its files?
