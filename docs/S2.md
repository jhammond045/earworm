# Stage S2: the modulation matrix

Built Nov 21, 2026, from [synth.md](synth.md) "Build plan > S2". Every source the design lists can now drive any value it may reach, through one shaper, and the Sound panel has a Patch view with a knob for every value and a Mod view with the matrix and the shaper editor. The patch structure is still the classic patch: routes are added and removed, modules are not.

## What was built

### Check 3 in detail

The voice filter's cutoff on a held note, moved by a layer route from the listening layer's sung features. Times are from the input step to the end of the 32-sample `process` call whose meters show the change.

| Input | Cutoff starts to move | Cutoff half way | Output level starts to move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Silence, then a sung "ah" (volume route) | 3.3 ms | 12.7 ms | 5.3 ms (half way 14.0 ms) |
| Silence, then a sung "ah" (pitch route) | 27.3 ms (the tier knows the pitch at 26.7 ms) | | |
| A held note moving up a minor third (pitch route) | 47.3 ms | 56.7 ms | |
| A held note moving up a fifth, 702 cents (pitch route) | 67.3 ms | 76.7 ms | |

The half-way times are longer than the first movement because a layer route's target is then smoothed with the value's own smoothing time (15 ms for the cutoff), as synth.md's step 4 says. A move of 700 cents or more waits for the capture tier's continuity guard (30 ms), which is older than S2.

### Check 4 in detail

The `routes` scene (`classic::bench_routes`) puts 48 voice routes on every voice patch of the M1 worst case, all moving every tick: the envelope gate, a vibrato, and 46 from the envelopes, LFOs, velocity and note pitch into the partial bank's spectrum (tilt, odd/even, formants, a band shape, fine tune), the filter (resonance, cutoff in octaves), the amp and the click; a third bent, a quarter with points, a fifth smoothed. They leave fold, clang and comb off, because switching those on in all 64 voices measures a heavier sound (S3's cost meter), not modulation.

| Run (best of 3, 10 s each) | Load | 48 routes | Heavy: also stretch, jitter, damp | Classic worst case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7.5 | 4.29x | 3.94x | 9.59x |
| 2 | 6.8 | 4.16x | 3.85x | 9.51x |

A profile of the 48-route scene: about a third of the time is route evaluation, 40% is the voices' control ticks (mostly the partial bank recomputing its spectrum because routes move its values every tick), a quarter is audio. Alone, 48 routes into the filter resonance cost about 8 ns each (8.6x against 11.8x with the envelope gate only). synth.md estimated routes at 2 to 5% of a core at the caps; with the partial bank's response included, the 48-route scene uses about 13% of a core more than the classic worst case.

## Where the build differs from the design

- **LFO waves are steps, not a morph.** `lfo.wave` takes six values (sine, triangle, saw, square, random steps, drift, at 0.2 apart), so the classic patch's 0 (sine) and 1 (drift) keep their meaning and a morph between two waves switches at its midpoint. LFOs have no beat sync and no free-running phase yet: both depend on the transport's Play-time reset (S5).
- **Envelopes have no curve.** The attack is linear and the decay and release exponential, as before. synth.md still lists a curve per envelope.
- **Layer routes.** They cannot be latched (there is no note), cannot target pitch (pitch is per voice), and cannot use the octaves target (that move happens after a voice maps its values). Pulls work in real units as voice pulls do. A layer route that moves another route's amount adds to that amount's normalized value, not by the source value in the amount's units as a voice route does.
- **Layer-route targets are smoothed** with each value's own smoothing time (15 ms for most), as step 4 says. It keeps a stepping macro or a jumpy sung feature from clicking, but it makes the sung volume's route to the cutoff reach half way after 12.7 ms rather than 4 ms. See the second question.
- **A smoothed layer route runs every tick**, like a route from a moving source, so its smoothing advances after a macro steps.
- **Routes are structure.** Adding or removing a route reloads the patch and silences the layer's sounding notes; S3 brings the 5 ms fade. Changing a route's shaper (range, bend, points, rise and fall, latch, polarity, mode, group, via) keeps the notes sounding.
- **Listen is a session setting**, like Arm: it is not saved with the project.
- **The mod wheel, bend and pressure** are sources in the engine and the matrix, set by `MSG_SET_PERF`, but nothing in the app moves them until S4 (MIDI and the on-screen stand-ins). Until then they sit at 0 (the bend at its centre).
- **A via from a bipolar source** (an LFO, voice or layer, or the bend) scales the amount by its distance from the centre.
- **Meters.** A voice route's output on the meters is the selected layer's loudest voice's. The selected layer's sung features are worked out every tick even when no route reads them, so the Mod view can show them.
- **Patch blob v2** (`EWP2`) adds the layer LFO list. The engine refuses v1 blobs; the app and the engine's classic patch write v2, and the M1 demo blobs were regenerated.
- **Pitch for a listening layer** is the tuned pitch, else the raw one, as for a voice. Measured, the tuner adds nothing to its delay with snap at 0.
- **The pitch route misses 26 ms by 1.3 ms** on this synthetic voice (check 3); the route itself adds one control tick.
- **Modulation costs more than synth.md estimated** (check 4): about 8 ns per route, and more when routes make the partial bank recompute every tick.

## Questions for the owner

1. The pitch route moves 27.3 ms after a sung onset; 26.7 ms of that is the live tier finding the pitch, unchanged since M2. Is that close enough to the 26 ms in the check, or should the live tier's pitch path be looked at?
2. Should a layer route's movement skip the target value's 15 ms smoothing? The sung volume would reach a cutoff about 9 ms sooner, at the risk of zipper noise from a stepping macro or a jumpy sung feature.
3. The 48-route benchmark counts routes into the spectrum, formants, filter, amp, click and pitch (4.2 to 4.3x). With stretch, jitter and damp also moving in every voice it drops to 3.85 to 3.94x on this loaded machine. Should S3's cost meter count routes that make the partial bank rebuild its tables every tick, or is that mix too unusual to plan for?
4. Check 5 needs someone new to the app to try the walkthrough "Make hardness set the attack" with a stopwatch.
5. LFO beat sync and free-running phase: build them with S5's Play-time reset, as planned here, or sooner?
