# Stage Q1: anchors and quantize without stretching

Built Nov 22, 2026, from [quant.md](quant.md) "Build plan > Q1". Each take now keeps the points in it that should land on the beat (its anchors), found right after the second pass. A clip can carry a Feel setting: a grid, triplets, a straight or swung feel, a strength and a catch window. When the track plays or exports, the compiler pulls the clip's anchors toward the grid and plays the notes, the hits and the take's curves through the time map that results. The stored notes and the take never change, so turning Feel off gives back the performance. The Quantize button now turns Feel on instead of moving the notes. Stretching the take's audio (Q2), one layer driving another (Q3) and anchor editing (Q4) are not in this stage.

### Anchor accuracy in detail

Errors are found minus true. A found anchor matches a true onset within 50 ms, one to one, nearest pairs first, as in the prototype.

| Takes | Found | Mean | Max | Where the error comes from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prototype drums A and B (`tests/anchors.rs`) | 87.5% | 1.18 ms | 8.3 ms | The eight quiet off-beat hats per loop the detector misses |
| Prototype sung line, detached and "da" starts | 100% | 1.26 ms | 5.0 ms | |
| Prototype sung line, legato joins | 100% | 10.6 ms | 26.7 ms | Late: pYIN's pitch trails a glide |
| `beat_corpus`, all hits | 98.7% | 2.71 ms | 36.2 ms | Early on almost every sound (median bias per take and sound -2.2 ms): kicks in the backbeat pattern -3.4 ms, voiced pops -8 ms on average and up to 26 ms in single takes, "nnt" -4 ms |
| `hum_corpus`, starts | 99.1% | 4.90 ms | 32.1 ms | Attacks of 6 to 30 ms, scoops and dips: detached starts 5.4 ms (bias -2.6), dips 6.4 ms, "da" 2.3 ms |
| `hum_corpus`, legato joins and slides | 96.9% | 16.8 ms | 42.6 ms | Late by about 12 ms on average, as in the prototype, on longer glides |

The onsets come from the second pass's own detector, which refines each onset to where the level has risen a fifth of the way (in dB) from the quietest point in the 40 ms before it to the peak after it. On these corpora it reads early on most sounds and spreads wider on the hums' soft attacks; joins are late because pYIN's pitch trails a glide. I have not traced each case further. The prototype's takes have short, clean attacks and meet the targets. The corpus tests now assert the levels measured (beats: 85% found and 3 ms; hums: 85% found, 6 ms for starts and 20 ms for joins), so a later change that makes them worse fails; see question 1.

## Where the build differs from the design

- **The time map repeats with the loop.** quant.md said the map is pinned at the loop's ends. Pinned, a note sung a little early on the first beat (stored just before the loop end, because placing a take wraps each note into the loop) could never reach that beat. Now an anchor's slot can be the next pass's first one, and its note starts at the top of the loop, reading its own take. quant.md is corrected.
- **The take audio's timing needed an engine change in Q1.** The engine reads a clip's curves and its take audio through one warp map. To move the curves with the notes and still play the audio at its performed timing, the v3 track blob's clip words 13 and 14 now name a separate audio warp run. Clips without Feel write zeros there, as before.
- **Beat clips' warp maps stay in the document.** The engine reads a beat clip's warp map only for its audio, which does not move in Q1, so it is not sent. Q2 sends it.
- **What Feel holds in Q1.** `Flex` has `on`, `grid`, `triplets`, `feel`, `strength`, `slotStrength` and `catch`. The audio mode (Q2), the layer and groove feels (Q3), and `sensitivity` and `edits` (Q4) come with their stages. The design's `Feel` type is `FlexFeel` here, since `Feel` already names a section's grid or free timing.
- **Swing is set on pairs of the grid**, and triplet slots are not swung.
- **The strength lane has no drawing in the page yet.** The compiler reads a `flex.strength` lane and the engine skips it, but the lane picker does not offer it; drawing it is Q4's.
- **Texture takes have no placement.** A texture take has no notes, so `placeTake` gives its clip no warp map, and its anchors cannot be placed. Such a clip uses its note starts (usually none), so Feel does nothing on it yet.
- **Takes recorded before Q1 have no anchors.** Their clips use their note or hit starts. Finding anchors again (the sensitivity control) is Q4.
- **Quantize in a free section now says it needs a grid.** It used to snap to 0.25 s there. The design gives free sections only the layer feel (Q3).
- **The timeline still draws notes where they were played.** Turning Feel on changes what you hear and the Feel row, not the drawing; see question 2.
- **The map costs 176 us per clip, not about a microsecond.** The microsecond was the prototype's Rust `quantize` alone; this is the TypeScript compile of a whole clip (anchors placed, notes and warp map moved), with simple linear searches. It runs when the track is compiled, not per audio block.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Anchor accuracy on the corpora.** The prototype's takes meet the targets; `beat_corpus` (2.7 ms) and `hum_corpus` (4.9 ms starts, 17 ms joins) do not: the second pass's onset refinement reads early on most of their sounds and spreads on soft attacks, and pYIN trails glides. Accept this for now and judge by ear in Q2, or improve the detector (an onset refinement made for anchors, a correction for the glide lag) before Q2?
2. **Drawing the played timing.** Should the timeline draw notes and hits where Feel plays them, with a faint mark where they were played (as the interface sketch shows for anchors), already in Q1, or wait for the anchor editing in Q4?
3. **S5's live-against-export check.** On `main` today it gives -171 dBFS instead of the bit-identical result S5 recorded; with Feel on it gives the same. Look into it as its own task?
4. **Quantize in free sections.** Keep refusing there until Q3 brings the layer feel, or keep the old destructive snap for free sections only?
