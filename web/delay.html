Round trip is input device plus output device. Total from voice to ear is input + engine + output, so total = round trip + engine internal delay. The engine test measures the M2 engine (klont, Live follow) unless the address ends in ?engine=m0. Device and engine delay are reported separately. Both mic tests need sound to get from the speakers to the mic: use a loopback cable or turn the speakers up. Keep headphones off the mic path, and use wired audio.
