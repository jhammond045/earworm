Into —

Your voice draws here while Capture is held.

Layers

Each layer is one instrument. A circle is a melody, a square a beat and a diamond a texture; a muted layer says mute. Capture, Record, Import, Sound and Perform all act on the selected layer.

Section

The section the pattern and timeline show; the stepper in the transport picks it. Grid counts beats at the tempo, Free counts seconds. Repeats is how many times it plays. Ramp is how long the tempo takes to arrive from the section before.

Track

Each lane is a layer in time: bars are written notes, ribbons are a take's pitch and loudness, blocks are hits on their kit slot's row. Drag to move, drag a note's right edge to change its length, double-click (or double-tap) empty space to add. Drag a hit up or down to another slot. Snap is the grid a drag lands on; Quantize turns on Feel for the lane. With the timeline focused, the arrows pick and Shift with an arrow moves.
Feel
Kit slots %

Feel plays the lane's notes or hits on a grid while the track plays, and keeps what you played underneath. Strength brings some of the played timing back; Catch is how far from a grid line a note is still pulled to it. Groove swings every other grid step. Audio says how a take's own sound is stretched to follow: Hits cuts it at each attack, Smooth stretches it evenly.

Presets

A meter cuts each bar into beats and plays them in the order you give, for every clip locked to it: 1 2 3 drops the last beat of a 4-beat bar, 1 2 3 3 plays the third beat twice, 1 3 4 2 shuffles the bar. Lock picks the meter for all of this layer's clips; Free plays them as written. Meter 1 also sets the section's length, the bar lines and the click's accent. The pattern belongs to this section. Press a beat to repeat it, drag it to move it, × to drop it, or type the numbers. Each cut falls on the clip's own hit nearest the beat, so a hit played a little early stays with its beat. A held sound in a dropped beat can ring on without its attack.

Morph
A marker moves the layer's sound to one of its states over Length, along Shape. Markers sit in the strip under each lane; drag one to move it. The list below names each marker's state; press one to remove it. A marker's notches count its state's number, so you can tell them apart without the colors.
Lane
The bottom strip of each lane is automation: one value of the layer over time. Pick what it shows here. Double-click the strip to add a point or remove one, and drag a point to move it. Record fills lanes from the knobs and controllers you move while it runs.

Sound

Simple is the preset, Tune, the states, the macros and how your voice and other sounds shape this layer. Patch shows every value of the layer's sound, module by module, and lets you add, remove and rewire them. Mod lists the routes that move those values. In Patch, the blue bar under a slider shows how far its routes can move it, and the mark where the sound has it now; pick a source under Modulate with, then press + beside a slider to route it there.
Layer
Tune
How a take's recorded pitch is shaped. Pitch snap pulls it to the scale; Speed is how fast it gets there; Vibrato keeps or flattens the wobble; Level evens the take's loudness: 0% keeps it as sung, and toward 100% quiet notes come up and loud ones come down. Slides says how a sung slide moves: Glide follows it, Gliss steps by semitones, Jump goes straight to the next note. A new take fills Root, Scale and the reference; Fill from take does it again.
States
A state is a full setting of the layer's sound. The preset, the macros and Influence edit the state picked here. Morph markers and Perform's scenes move the layer from one state to another. Up to eight.
Macros
Eight named moves on the state you are editing, on top of the preset. The sound follows while you drag; letting go is one undo step.
Gestures
How far your voice pushes this layer while it plays live: how loud, how open, how hard, how breathy, and the mouth's shape. On a beat layer, Follow is how far a hit follows the sung shape.
Kit slot
One hit, from the mic or a file, becomes the picked slot: the match treats it as a decaying sound. Hear slot plays it. Hold Teach and repeat one sound: the next beat take groups its hits with what you taught.

Influence
Move this state toward a sound: choose a file, or hold Listen and make the sound. The meters show how far each aspect would move. Amount and the aspect boxes decide how much of Tone, Shape, Motion, Grit and Space is used; Commit writes the blend into the state. Saved lists sounds kept on the Influence page. Play source plays the file; Hold note plays this layer at the source's pitch.

Motion is provisional. A longer sound settles it.

Perform

Play the selected layer. The computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play an octave from C, and Z and X move the octave; on a beat layer A S D F play the four kit slots. Listen lets the mic drive the layer's sung features while the notes come from the keys or MIDI; Arm makes your voice play it. Record in the transport records what you play here.

Mic timing

Align is the round trip from the speakers back to the mic, in milliseconds. A take is placed that much earlier, so a note sung on the beat lands on it. 30 ms is a guess; Measure plays five chirps and times them. Use headphones. If the audio device changes, Capture pauses until you measure again.
