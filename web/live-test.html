Live follow listening test

Wear wired headphones. Open Live follow in another tab (or both side by side),Live follow is on the right: press Start, then work down this list. Settings to use are in each step. Answers save in this browser as you go.

To judge the sound without hearing it while you sing, use Record a take on the Live follow page: Record, sing the step, Stop, then Play synth. Change a setting and Play synth again to hear the same take another way.
